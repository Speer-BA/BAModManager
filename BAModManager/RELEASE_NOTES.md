# Broken Arrow Mod Manager v1.0.0

First release.

**Download `BAModManager-v1.0.0.zip` below, unzip it and run `BAModManager.exe`.** No installer and no .NET needed.

> [!WARNING]
> **DO NOT play Broken Arrow with Anti-Cheat turned on while MelonLoader is installed** - Anti-Cheat can detect MelonLoader and mods, and that can get your account banned. Use Steam's *Play Anti-Cheat Disabled* launch option, and uninstall MelonLoader before normal online matches. Details in the README.

### What it does
- Installs, updates and uninstalls MelonLoader in one click
- PLAY always starts the game with Anti-Cheat disabled while MelonLoader is installed
- Installs `.bamod` packages, `.dll` mods, `.bundle` models and `.zip` mod packs by drag & drop or double-click
- Switch mods on/off, remove them (to the Recycle Bin), see their picture, description and files
- Mod requirements: warnings for missing or outdated mods, with download links
- *Create package* tool for modders
- English, Deutsch, Français, Русский, 中文

### Good to know
- Windows SmartScreen may warn that the app is unrecognised (it isn't code-signed): **More info → Run anyway**.
- Keep `Inter-LICENSE.txt` next to the exe if you share it.
